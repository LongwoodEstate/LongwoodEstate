The House

A century in the making

Named after Napoleon's final home on St Helena, Longwood was established as a sheep station in the 1850s before the present house — designed in the manner of Lutyens — was completed in 1921. Its gracious neo-Georgian interior, ornate ceilings, Adam fireplaces, and grand staircase make it one of New Zealand's most distinguished private homes.

Longwood offers itself to a small number of weddings, private stays, and corporate retreats each year — always by arrangement, always exclusively yours.

The Grounds

Vast lawns, venerable trees

The estate's park-like grounds have been a century in the making — ancient English oaks, a Sunken Garden, a reflecting pool, and the quiet drama of Napoleon's own oak, grown from a cutting of the original.

  • Sunken garden & formal terraces
  • Tennis court & croquet lawn
  • Swimming pool & poolhouse
  • Historic stables & courtyard taproom
  • Napoleon's Oak — grown from St Helena
  • Nine-hole heritage golf course
